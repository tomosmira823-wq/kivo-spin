## Kivo Spin 1.0

First release. Drag any file, hold Shift, and drop it on the wheel.

- Convert between PNG, JPG, WEBP, HEIC, AVIF, TIFF, BMP, ICO, PDF and DOCX
- Tools: compress, metadata, edit, annotate, remove background, add background, crop, redact, combine
- Works offline – background removal runs on your PC
- Updates itself
